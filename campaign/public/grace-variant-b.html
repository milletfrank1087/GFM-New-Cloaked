The Story

In April 2026, my vibrant, 21-year-old sister Grace was diagnosed with Glioblastoma Multiforme (Grade IV) — the most aggressive and malignant form of primary brain cancer.

Just weeks before her diagnosis, Grace was finishing her third year of medical school at the University of Manchester. She dedicated her life to healing others, spending long nights studying clinical pathology and volunteering on weekends. Then, sudden excruciating morning headaches and visual field loss revealed a 4.2cm lesion in her right parietal lobe.

The Standard of Care & Its Severe Limitations

Grace underwent immediate craniotomy and partial surgical resection at Salford Royal Hospital in May 2026, followed by the rigorous Stupp Protocol (concurrent chemoradiation with Temozolomide).

However, molecular pathology confirmed her tumor possesses an unmethylated MGMT promoter, meaning standard temozolomide chemotherapy has less than a 15% efficacy response rate. On the NHS standard pathway, the median survival prognosis for unmethylated glioblastoma is merely 12 to 14 months.

The Lifesaving Alternative: IOZK Immunotherapy

We have been accepted by the renowned IOZK (Immuno-Oncological Centre) in Cologne, Germany, led by Dr. Stefaan Van Gool. Their advanced personalized protocol combines:

  • IO-VAC® Autologous Dendritic Cell Vaccines: Engineered from Grace's exact tumor antigens to train her T-cells to identify residual glioblastoma cells.
  • Newcastle Disease Virus (NDV): Oncolytic virus therapy targeting stem-like tumor cells without damaging healthy brain tissue.
  • Modulated Electro-Hyperthermia (mEHT): Sensitizing tumor cells to immunotherapy.

Why We Urgently Need Your Help

Because this treatment takes place in Germany, it is not funded by the NHS. The complete 6-cycle immunotherapy protocol, specialized lab antigen processing, MRI monitoring, and medical travel total £90,000.

Every single pound brings Grace closer to Germany, closer to receiving her custom vaccines, and closer to a future where she can finish medical school and save lives herself.

From the bottom of our hearts, thank you for standing with Grace and our family.
