# Ad creatives: Canada, Australia, Poland

Same proven structure as the live Italy / Portugal / Spain ads ("In an instant, everything changed").
Every fact matches the landing page (diagnosis October, seizure during a lecture, hospital, university,
public system, goal, currency). Image: the same before/after photo used by the live ads.

How to publish (while the "Comments" app is in development mode):
1. Meta Business Suite → RiseHope Page → create the post **as a link post / ad-ready post** with the image, text, headline, **Donate now** button and the URL below
   (or Ads Manager → create ad → "Create post" with the same fields).
2. Send Claude the post link or ID → the ad gets attached to the paused campaign via the API.

## 🇨🇦 Canada: Olivia (English, CAD)

**URL:** https://cag.risehope.online/  ·  **Button:** Donate now
**Headline:** Medical Student with a Brain Tumour: Help Olivia.

**Primary text:**

In an instant, everything changed.

On the left, Olivia: 21, a medical student at the University of Toronto, dreaming of becoming a neuro-oncologist. On the right, her reality today: Olivia is fighting a grade IV brain tumour.

In October, after a seizure during a lecture at medical school, Olivia was diagnosed with glioblastoma, the most aggressive malignant brain tumour. Standard treatment gives a median survival of 14 months.

Let's be clear: her surgery, radiotherapy and chemotherapy at Toronto Western Hospital were covered by OHIP. That part is taken care of.

What is NOT covered, by OHIP or any public health plan, is dendritic cell immunotherapy in Germany. A treatment that clinical studies show increases survival by 68%. It is classed as an "innovative therapy" and no public or private plan reimburses it. We need CA$135,000.

In her own words: "I wanted to become a doctor to help people. Now I'm learning what it means to be the patient — and how unbearable it is to know a treatment exists that could save you, but you can't reach it."

Every dollar is time. Please don't let her face this alone.
